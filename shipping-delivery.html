AERA Policies

Shipping & Delivery

Everything you need to know about order preparation, delivery areas, shipping fees, and order tracking.

1. Order Confirmation & Processing

Retail orders are confirmed once payment verification is completed. Standard retail orders are packed within 3–5 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding Philippine public holidays).

Once your parcel is handed over to our courier partner, you can track its status and courier waybill reference anytime on our Track My Order page using your Order ID and checkout email.

2. Standard Retail Shipping Rates

Shipping fees are calculated automatically at checkout based on your delivery region:

Delivery AreaStandard Rate
Metro Manila (all cities)₱80
Greater Metro Manila / Luzon (Cavite, Rizal, Bulacan & other Luzon)₱150
Visayas₱180
Mindanao₱200

Please ensure your delivery address and mobile number are complete and accurate so our courier can contact you without delay.

3. Order Concerns & Damaged Items

Please inspect your parcel upon delivery. If you receive a damaged, leaking, or incorrect item, contact us within 7 days of delivery at contactaeraofficial@gmail.com with your Order ID, description of the issue, and clear photos (and unboxing video if available).

4. Reseller Shipments

For AERA Circle reseller orders, shipping and delivery terms—including 2-business-day preparation after 50% down payment verification, Metro Manila same-day courier options, and provincial bulk freight—are governed by Section 5 of the AERA Reseller Terms & Conditions.
