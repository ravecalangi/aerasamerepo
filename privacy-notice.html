AERA Policies

Privacy Notice

How AERA collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information across our online store and AERA Circle reseller program.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the personal information you voluntarily provide to process your orders, respond to your inquiries, or review your AERA Circle reseller application. This may include:

  • Your full name, email address, mobile number, and delivery/billing address;
  • Payment confirmation details and order records;
  • For AERA Circle reseller applicants: business information, selling channels, valid government ID images for identity verification, and a 2×2 profile photo for your private reseller portal account.

2. How We Use & Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We use your details strictly to fulfill orders, verify reseller applications, send order or program updates, and provide customer support.

Information is shared only with authorized AERA personnel, delivery couriers, or payment/service providers where reasonably necessary to complete your order or verification, or when required by Philippine law.

3. Retention & Security

We retain personal information and verification documents only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, applicable recordkeeping requirements, and account support. Access to reseller verification IDs is strictly limited to authorized AERA personnel.

4. Your Privacy Rights & Contact

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw your consent (subject to lawful retention requirements), anytime by emailing contactaeraofficial@gmail.com.
