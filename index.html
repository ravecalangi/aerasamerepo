About Us

A little pleasure, every day.

AERA is a beauty and personal care brand that brings a little pleasure into everyday routines through scent and body care. Thoughtful, playful and approachable, it is made for ordinary moments that feel good enough to linger in.

Made for everyday moments

AERA is built for the small rituals that make a day feel good — the spray before you head out, the soap that smells just right, the serum that actually sinks in. Simple things, done well.

Why AERA exists

Good body care shouldn’t feel like a chore or cost a fortune. AERA was created to bring a little pleasure into ordinary routines with mists, soaps and a serum that are easy to love, easy to use and made for our warm, humid weather.

What “Stay a little longer” means

It’s the feeling we want to create: a routine, a scent or a quiet moment that’s good enough to make you pause and stay a little longer.

AERA Circle

Join the Circle.

AERA Circle is our reseller program for individuals and businesses who want to share AERA with their own community. Applying and ordering are separate from regular retail shopping.

1st Tier

For new resellers starting out.

  • Up to 15% off retail prices
  • Starter kit with product samples
  • Minimum net PO: ₱6,000

2nd Tier

For active sellers building a steady customer base.

  • Up to 20% off retail prices
  • Priority restocks on new scents
  • Minimum net PO: ₱15,000

3rd Tier

For high-volume sellers and bulk buyers.

  • Up to 30% off retail prices
  • Early access to new launches
  • Minimum net PO: ₱25,000

Bulk orders

For purchases over ₱50,000, email us at contactaeraofficial@gmail.com so we can assist you directly.

Ordering expectations

Apply through the form, and once approved you’ll receive the reseller price list. Orders are placed directly with our team, packed within 3–5 business days and paid in full before shipping via bank transfer or e-wallet.

Policy notes

Resellers agree to keep prices at or above AERA’s minimum advertised price, to use official product photos and descriptions, and not to make medical claims about any product.
