    Ordering policy

    No cancellations or refunds. Once your order is placed, it can't be cancelled, changed or refunded. Please review your items and delivery details first.

    Payment. Orders are processed only after we verify your payment. Upload a clear screenshot of your receipt showing the amount, date and reference number.

    Shipping. Orders are packed within 3–5 business days. Shipping fees are based on your delivery area. Make sure your address and phone number are correct and reachable.

    Damaged or wrong items. Contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos or an unboxing video so we can help.

    Product use. AERA products are for external cosmetic use. Do a patch test first if you have sensitive skin and stop use if irritation occurs.
