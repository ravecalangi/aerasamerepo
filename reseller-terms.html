3. Payment terms

A 50% down payment of the confirmed net merchandise value is required when placing an order. Processing begins once AERA has confirmed the order and verified the down payment.

The remaining 50%, together with any delivery charges payable to AERA, must be settled and verified before dispatch. Charges payable directly to the courier will be identified separately.

AERA will provide the payment instructions and balance payment deadline in the order confirmation. Orders will not be released for delivery until the required payment has been completed.

4. Cancellation and Down Payments

Please review all products, variants, quantities, and prices carefully before confirming your order and paying the required 50% down payment.

Cancellation requests must be submitted through AERA’s official contact channel. The applicable cancellation terms depend on the order’s status when AERA receives the request.

Before processing begins:

The order may be cancelled, and all payments received for the order will be refunded in full.

After processing begins but before the order is ready for dispatch:

The order may be cancelled, subject to a cancellation charge equal to 50% of the required down payment, equivalent to 25% of the confirmed net merchandise value. Any payment received beyond this charge will be refunded.

For example, for an order with a net merchandise value of ₱6,000, the required down payment is ₱3,000. The cancellation charge would be ₱1,500, and the remaining ₱1,500 of the down payment would be refunded.

Once the order is ready for dispatch:

Voluntary cancellations and change-of-mind refunds are no longer accepted. The reseller remains responsible for settling any outstanding balance before the order is released for delivery.

Order status and notifications

“Processing” begins when AERA starts preparing or packing the confirmed order.

“Ready for dispatch” means the order has been fully packed, checked, and labeled for delivery, and AERA has sent the reseller written notice confirming this status. A courier booking alone does not establish that an order is ready for dispatch.

AERA will communicate processing and dispatch-readiness updates through its official contact channel. Cancellation requests received before the dispatch-readiness notice will be assessed under the earlier applicable stage.

The stated order preparation period does not constitute a free cancellation window. Cancellation terms depend on the actual order status described above.

Refund timeline

Refunds due under this policy will be processed within three (3) business days after AERA confirms the cancellation and receives complete and accurate refund account details.

AERA will provide confirmation once the refund has been sent. Any additional bank or payment-provider posting time is outside AERA’s processing period.

Business days are Monday to Friday, 9AM to 5PM, excluding Philippine public holidays.

Exceptions and applicable rights

These restrictions apply to voluntary, change-of-mind cancellations. They do not limit remedies for defective, incorrect, or otherwise nonconforming products, or where AERA cannot fulfill its obligations.

If AERA cannot supply an item, the reseller may choose an agreed substitute or a refund of payments attributable to that item. No cancellation charge applies to an item cancelled because AERA cannot supply it.

All cancellation charges and refund arrangements remain subject to applicable law.

5. Shipping and Delivery

Order preparation

Orders are prepared within two (2) business days after AERA confirms the order and verifies the required 50% down payment. The confirmation date is Day 0; the next business day is Day 1.

For unusually large orders, AERA will communicate any longer preparation timeline before accepting payment. If an unexpected delay occurs, AERA will promptly inform the reseller and provide an updated estimate.

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding Philippine public holidays.

Payment before dispatch

The remaining balance and any delivery charges payable to AERA must be fully settled and verified before dispatch. Charges payable directly to the courier will be identified separately.

The preparation period does not include time spent awaiting the reseller’s balance payment or courier transit time.

Shipping fees and delivery methods

Shipping fees are charged separately and do not count toward the initial or monthly minimum net purchase requirement.

Within Metro Manila, same-day courier delivery may be arranged once the order is ready and full payment has been verified, subject to booking cutoffs and courier availability. Same-day delivery refers to the courier service and does not mean orders will be prepared on the day they are placed.

Provincial and bulk orders may be delivered through land freight, cargo trucking, or another suitable service, subject to destination, shipment size, and carrier acceptance.

AERA will confirm the delivery arrangement and applicable charges before booking. Delivery times are estimates unless expressly guaranteed in writing.

Delivery details and receipt

Resellers must provide complete and accurate delivery details and ensure that an authorized recipient is available to receive the shipment.

Any additional delivery costs resulting from incorrect details, an unavailable recipient, or a reseller-requested change will be communicated to the reseller and supported by the courier’s applicable charges.

Please inspect your order upon receipt and report missing, incorrect, leaking, or visibly damaged items promptly through AERA’s official contact channel. Include your order reference and available supporting photos.

An unboxing video may assist with assessment but is not required as the sole proof of a concern. AERA will assist in investigating shipment issues and coordinating with the courier as appropriate.

6. Product concerns, returns, and exchanges

Reporting and supporting evidence

Resellers must report missing items, incorrect products, spillage, leakage, breakage, or suspected defects promptly through AERA’s official contact channel. Please provide:

  • The order reference and affected products and quantities;
  • A description of the concern;
  • Clear photos of the affected products and the inner and outer packaging;
  • Photos of the shipping label and batch details, where relevant; and
  • Any available delivery or unboxing footage.

AERA may request additional information reasonably necessary to assess the claim. An unboxing video is helpful but is not the sole acceptable evidence.

Keep affected products and packaging available for assessment where reasonably safe and practical. Do not return or dispose of them until AERA provides instructions, unless retaining them would present a safety risk.

Pre-shipment documentation

AERA will inspect orders and provide photos documenting their visible condition and packing before shipment. These records will be considered together with the reseller’s evidence and any courier findings when assessing a concern.

Replacement of verified concerns

Replacement is AERA’s standard remedy for verified product defects and fulfillment errors attributable to AERA. Missing items will be supplied, and incorrect or defective items will be replaced at no additional product cost.

AERA will cover reasonable return and replacement delivery costs arranged or approved by AERA for these verified concerns.

Replacement availability and the expected dispatch schedule will be communicated following assessment. Any alternative product requires the reseller’s agreement.

Refunds are not AERA’s standard remedy for these claims. However, this policy does not exclude refunds or other remedies required by applicable law, or an agreed resolution where AERA cannot provide the appropriate replacement.

Exclusions

Unsold stock, slow sales, changes of mind, and personal scent preferences do not qualify for replacement unless AERA agrees otherwise in writing.

Damage established to have resulted from improper storage, handling, alteration, or misuse by the reseller is not covered by AERA’s replacement policy.

Transit-related concerns will be assessed under Section 5. Voluntary cancellations are governed by Section 4. Nothing in this section limits rights or remedies available under applicable law.

Transit damage and courier claims

AERA will inspect and securely pack orders using packaging appropriate to the products and agreed delivery method.

Loss or damage established to have occurred solely through courier handling after proper handover will be addressed through the applicable courier claims process, subject to the agreed shipping terms and applicable law. AERA will provide available packing photos, dispatch records, and reasonable assistance with the claim.

Pre-shipment photos do not automatically determine responsibility. Damage attributable to an existing product defect, incorrect fulfillment, or inadequate packaging by AERA remains subject to AERA’s assessment and replacement policy.

Any agreement allocating transit risk to the reseller must be disclosed and accepted before shipment. It does not remove responsibility attributable to AERA’s own acts or omissions.

7. Responsible selling and product care

Resellers are responsible for maintaining the condition, quality, and authenticity of AERA products while the products are in their possession.

Original packaging and product integrity

Products must be sold in their original AERA packaging, with labels, batch details, directions, and safety information intact.

Resellers must not decant, dilute, mix, refill, repackage, relabel, or otherwise alter AERA products without AERA’s prior written approval.

Products designated as testers or samples must not be sold as new retail products.

Proper storage and handling

Resellers must follow the storage instructions and warnings provided on product labels or communicated by AERA.

Products must be kept in clean, dry, and secure conditions, protected from direct sunlight, excessive heat, moisture, contamination, and other conditions that may affect their quality. Products carrying flammability warnings must be kept away from flames and ignition sources.

Products must be handled carefully to prevent breakage, leakage, damaged seals, or contamination.

Stock monitoring

Resellers must monitor product condition, expiry dates, and other applicable product-use information. Earlier-expiring stock should be sold first (FIFO).

Expired, leaking, opened, tampered, contaminated, or otherwise damaged products must not be sold as new or suitable for use. Affected stock must be separated from saleable inventory while awaiting assessment or appropriate disposal instructions.

Delivery to customers

When arranging delivery to their own customers, resellers must use protective packaging appropriate to the products and follow the carrier’s applicable restrictions.

Resellers are responsible for damage attributable to their own inadequate packaging, improper storage, or mishandling. Product defects and concerns relating to AERA’s original fulfillment remain subject to Section 6.

Safety concerns and recalls

Resellers must promptly notify AERA of suspected product safety concerns, including reported adverse reactions, and provide available product and batch details.

Expected changes specifically described in AERA’s product information packet—such as gradual color deepening in certain vanilla-containing formulations—do not, on their own, constitute a product defect or safety concern, provided the change falls within the conditions and characteristics documented for that product.

Changes that differ from AERA’s guidance, or are accompanied by an unusual odor, unexpected separation, leakage, irritation, or other signs of deterioration, must still be reported. Any reported adverse reaction must be reported regardless of whether the product’s appearance matches a documented expected change.

8. Product information and customer service

Accurate product information

Resellers must use accurate product descriptions, directions, warnings, and claims provided or approved by AERA.

Product labels and the latest AERA product information packet must serve as the primary references when answering customer questions. Resellers must not remove important qualifications, exaggerate benefits, guarantee results, or make unverified medical or therapeutic claims.

Product names do not authorize additional claims beyond those expressly provided or approved by AERA.

Expected product changes

Resellers should explain expected product characteristics and changes using AERA’s documented guidance. These may include gradual color deepening in certain formulations, where specifically identified in the product information packet.

Resellers must not assume that every change in appearance, scent, or texture is normal. Changes outside AERA’s documented guidance and any reported adverse reactions must be referred to AERA in accordance with Section 7.

Clear communication with customers

Resellers must communicate prices, product sizes, availability, payment arrangements, delivery charges, estimated delivery schedules, and applicable after-sales policies clearly before accepting a customer’s order.

Products that are not on hand must be clearly identified as preorder or subject to availability. Resellers must not promise delivery dates, promotions, or benefits on AERA’s behalf without authorization.

Resellers are responsible for their own customer transactions and must not present themselves as AERA’s head office or a company-owned store.

Customer concerns and complaints

Resellers must respond to customer inquiries and complaints respectfully and within a reasonable time.

Concerns involving suspected defects, incorrect products, leakage, breakage, or product safety must be documented and promptly referred to AERA when its assistance is required. Relevant order details, batch information, and available supporting evidence should be included.

Resellers must not dismiss an adverse reaction solely because the product appears normal or because a documented color change has occurred.

AERA’s reseller replacement and cancellation policies govern transactions between AERA and the reseller. Resellers must not represent these policies as removing any rights their own retail customers may have under applicable law.

Customer information

Resellers must protect customer information and collect or share only what is reasonably necessary to fulfill orders, resolve concerns, or meet applicable requirements.

When referring a concern to AERA, resellers must share customer information through an appropriate private channel and obtain consent where required. Customer names, contact details, photographs, and complaint records must not be posted publicly without appropriate permission or another lawful basis.

Reseller purchase prices

Reseller purchase prices are based on AERA’s applicable product prices and the reseller’s approved tier, subject to the purchase requirements stated in these terms.

AERA will confirm the applicable prices and discounts before accepting payment. Any future price changes will be communicated before new orders are confirmed and will not retroactively change an already confirmed and paid order without agreement.

Suggested retail prices

AERA provides suggested retail prices as a reference. Resellers independently determine their retail selling prices, subject to applicable law.

Resellers must communicate their actual selling prices and any additional charges clearly. Price comparisons, discount claims, and promotional savings must be accurate and must not mislead customers.

Reseller promotions

Resellers may offer promotions at their own expense, provided the promotion uses accurate product information and does not misleadingly suggest that AERA sponsors or funds it.

Discounts offered by a reseller do not change the amount payable to AERA or reduce the reseller’s minimum purchase requirements.

Resellers are responsible for clearly stating their promotion’s duration, inclusions, eligibility requirements, stock limitations, and other applicable conditions, and for honoring their advertised offers.

AERA promotions

Participation in AERA-organized campaigns is subject to the conditions communicated for each campaign.

Reseller discounts cannot be combined with other AERA discounts or promotional offers unless AERA expressly confirms that the offers may be combined.

Resellers must not independently promise AERA-funded gifts, discounts, reimbursements, or other benefits. Campaign materials must be used within their stated validity period and according to AERA’s instructions.

Selling channels

Resellers must disclose their intended selling channels during application and notify AERA of material changes, including the addition of online shops, marketplace accounts, or physical retail locations.

All listings must accurately identify the reseller’s own shop or business and describe authentic AERA products using current product information.

Resellers must follow the applicable rules of their selling platforms, including product listing, shipping, advertising, and customer service requirements.

Reseller approval does not grant exclusive rights to a platform, location, or territory unless separately agreed in writing.

Brand representation

Resellers must not describe their shop as AERA’s head office, a company-owned store, or an exclusive distributor unless specifically authorized in writing.

Use of AERA’s name, photographs, logos, and other marketing materials remains subject to Section 10.

During their approved participation in the reseller program, resellers may use the product photographs, descriptions, and ready-to-use promotional materials supplied by AERA to promote authentic AERA products.

Some supplied materials may already contain the AERA logo. Permission to use these materials does not grant permission to extract, reproduce, or use the logo separately.

AERA does not provide resellers with standalone logo files for independent use.

Restrictions on logo use

The logo appearing naturally on authentic product packaging in a reseller’s own photographs or videos is permitted.

Proper use of supplied materials

AERA-provided materials may be resized proportionally to fit a platform, provided they remain clear and complete.

Resellers must not crop out or obscure branding, distort product images, remove important information, or change product claims or promotional conditions.

Only materials designated by AERA for reseller use may be used. Materials published on AERA’s website or social media are not automatically approved for independent reproduction.

Reseller identity and original content

Resellers must use their own shop name and identity. They may identify the products they sell as AERA products and describe themselves as an authorized AERA reseller while their authorization remains active.

Resellers must not present their accounts or stores as AERA’s head office, a company-owned store, or an exclusive distributor without written authorization.

Business names, usernames, domains, and marketplace store names incorporating AERA’s name require prior written approval.

Resellers may create their own product photographs and videos, provided they follow Sections 8 and 9 and do not add AERA’s logo separately. Any reuse of reseller-created content by AERA requires the creator’s permission or a separate agreement.

Ownership and corrections

Permission to use supplied materials is nonexclusive, cannot be transferred to another business or person, and does not transfer ownership of AERA’s brand materials.

Resellers must promptly correct or remove outdated, inaccurate, expired, or unauthorized materials when notified by AERA.

When reseller participation ends

Once authorization ends, resellers must stop presenting themselves as currently authorized AERA resellers and discontinue using supplied promotional materials unless AERA agrees otherwise in writing.

Any continued sale of authentic remaining stock will follow the arrangements confirmed under Section 13. Listings must accurately describe the products without implying continuing reseller authorization.

Approved resellers purchase AERA products for resale and operate their own businesses independently.

Participation in the reseller program does not create an employment relationship, legal partnership, joint venture, franchise, or agency relationship with AERA.

Resellers manage their own selling activities, operating expenses, customer transactions, and business decisions, subject to these terms and applicable law.

No authority to act on AERA’s behalf

Resellers may not enter into contracts, incur expenses, accept obligations, or make commitments on behalf of AERA without prior written authorization.

Payments collected by resellers from their customers are payments to the reseller’s own business. Resellers must not represent these as payments received directly by AERA.

Business requirements

Resellers are responsible for obtaining and maintaining registrations, permits, tax compliance, and other requirements applicable to their own operations. Approval by AERA does not replace any registration, permit, or authorization required to operate the reseller’s business.

Nonexclusive participation

Reseller approval does not grant exclusive rights to a territory, customer group, selling platform, or location unless AERA expressly agrees otherwise in writing. AERA may sell directly to customers and approve other resellers operating in the same areas or channels.

Sales and earnings

AERA does not guarantee sales volume, profit, earnings, or recovery of the reseller’s investment. Reseller discounts represent reductions from AERA’s suggested retail prices and do not guarantee a corresponding net profit. Actual results depend on the reseller’s selling prices, expenses, customer demand, and business activities.

Resellers should assess their ability to meet the initial and monthly purchase requirements before selecting a tier.

Schedules, participation arrangements, and availability will be communicated separately. Priority access does not guarantee stock availability. Shipping fees are charged separately unless a specific promotion expressly states otherwise.

When a purchase counts

An order counts toward purchase requirements only once AERA has received and verified full payment of its net merchandise value. A down payment alone does not qualify the order for purchase counting.

The date AERA verifies full merchandise payment is the qualifying purchase date. Shipping charges do not count toward purchase qualification, although applicable delivery charges must still be settled as required before dispatch.

Cancelled orders will be removed from qualifying purchase totals. Refunded merchandise will be deducted from the relevant totals. AERA will communicate any resulting adjustment to tier qualification or incentive rankings.

Joining month and enrollment cutoff

The qualifying purchase date of the initial order determines the reseller’s first qualifying month:

  • Initial purchases qualifying from the 1st through the 15th satisfy the requirement for that calendar month.
  • Initial purchases qualifying from the 16th through the last day of the month satisfy the requirement for the following calendar month. No separate minimum is required for the partial joining month.
For example, an initial qualifying purchase dated January 10 satisfies January’s requirement, and the next monthly requirement begins February 1. An initial qualifying purchase dated January 20 satisfies February’s requirement, and the next monthly requirement begins March 1.

The initial purchase is counted once only. This enrollment cutoff does not change the counting period for ordinary reorders.

Monthly purchase requirements

After the first qualifying month, resellers must meet their selected tier’s minimum net purchase requirement each calendar month.

Resellers may place smaller orders throughout the month and combine qualifying purchases to reach the monthly minimum. Each individual reorder does not need to meet the full monthly minimum.

Ordinary reorders count toward the calendar month in which full merchandise payment is verified, subject to the grace-period allocation rules below.

Monthly periods run from the first through the last day of each calendar month, using Philippine time.

Ten-day grace period

A reseller who falls short of the monthly minimum will receive a grace period covering the first ten (10) calendar days of the following month to complete the shortfall.

AERA will communicate the shortfall and applicable deadline in writing. Full merchandise payment for purchases used to complete the shortfall must be verified within the grace period.

Purchases allocated to the previous month’s shortfall will not also count toward the new month’s requirement. Any qualifying amount above that shortfall will count toward the new month.

Tier adjustments

If the shortfall remains after the grace period, AERA may offer a lower tier based on the reseller’s qualifying purchases.

Any tier adjustment requires the reseller’s written acceptance. AERA will confirm the new discount, monthly purchase requirement, and effective date before applying the change. Tier adjustments will not retroactively change prices on previously confirmed orders.

Inactive reseller status

If the reseller does not complete the requirement during the grace period and no tier adjustment is agreed, their reseller status will become inactive upon written notice.

Inactive status is an administrative pause in tier discounts and benefits tied to active reseller status. It does not cancel existing orders, payment obligations, or valid product claims.

Continued participation

13. Suspension or termination

Grounds for suspension or termination

AERA may suspend or terminate reseller participation for material breaches of these terms, including:

  • Fraud, falsified documents, or deliberately misleading application information;
  • Selling counterfeit, tampered, altered, expired, or otherwise unsafe products;
  • Misusing AERA’s name, brand materials, or reseller authorization;
  • Making misleading product claims or unauthorized commitments on AERA’s behalf;
  • Failing to settle payments according to confirmed order arrangements; or
  • Repeatedly failing to correct a breach after receiving written notice.

Failure to meet monthly purchase requirements will ordinarily be handled through the grace period, tier adjustment, and inactive status provisions in Section 12.

Notice and opportunity to respond

Where reasonably practicable, AERA will provide written notice describing the concern, any temporary restrictions, and the steps and deadline for responding or correcting the issue.

AERA will consider the reseller’s response and available evidence before confirming its decision in writing.

Immediate suspension may be necessary where there is a credible concern involving fraud, product safety, counterfeit products, or ongoing misuse of AERA’s identity. A temporary suspension does not, by itself, establish that a breach has occurred.

Effect of suspension

During suspension, AERA may temporarily pause acceptance of new reseller orders, access to tier discounts, use of supplied promotional materials, and benefits tied to active reseller status.

The written notice will explain the restrictions, treatment of pending orders, and any conditions for restoring participation. Resellers must immediately stop selling any products specifically identified as unsafe or subject to investigation or recall.

Voluntary withdrawal

Resellers may end their participation by notifying AERA through its official contact channel. Withdrawal does not automatically cancel confirmed orders or create a right to return unsold stock. Order cancellations remain subject to Section 4, and product concerns remain subject to Section 6.

Effect of termination

Once participation ends, the reseller must stop presenting themselves as currently authorized by AERA and discontinue use of supplied promotional materials unless AERA agrees otherwise in writing.

AERA will confirm arrangements for outstanding orders and any continued sale of authentic, fully paid remaining stock. Products identified as unsafe, recalled, expired, tampered, or otherwise unsuitable for sale must not be sold. Termination does not automatically require AERA to repurchase unsold stock.

Outstanding obligations and product claims

Suspension, withdrawal, or termination does not automatically cancel existing payment obligations, confirmed orders, valid product claims, or responsibilities concerning customer information, brand materials, and product safety.

AERA will communicate how pending orders will be fulfilled or otherwise resolved. Payments will not be automatically forfeited solely because reseller participation has ended. Any cancellation charge or refund will be assessed under the applicable order terms and law.

Return to the reseller program

Reinstatement after suspension or termination requires AERA’s written approval and may depend on correction of the breach and completion of any stated conditions.

The reduced reactivation purchase requirement in Section 12 applies to inactivity caused by missed monthly purchase requirements. It does not automatically apply to suspension or termination for a breach. Nothing in this section limits either party’s rights or remedies under applicable law.

15. Changes to these terms

15. Changes to these terms

Notice of changes

AERA may update these terms as the reseller program develops or as operational or legal requirements change.

Material changes will be communicated in writing through the reseller’s registered email address or another agreed official contact channel before they take effect. The notice will identify the changes, the updated version, and the effective date.

Material changes include changes to reseller discounts, purchase requirements, payment arrangements, cancellation policies, benefits, or conditions for continued participation.

Existing orders and earned benefits

Changes will not retroactively alter confirmed orders, payments already made, completed qualifying purchases, or benefits already earned under an announced program without the reseller’s agreement, except where required by applicable law.

Changes to monthly purchase requirements will apply from a future monthly period identified in the notice. Resellers will not be required to meet a higher minimum for a period that has already begun without their agreement.

Review and acceptance

Resellers will have an opportunity to review material changes before they apply. AERA will request written or electronic acknowledgment and acceptance of updated terms where they change the reseller’s agreed obligations.

Silence alone will not be treated as acceptance of a material change.

If a reseller does not accept a material change, AERA and the reseller will confirm arrangements for continued participation under an agreed alternative or for ending participation under Section 13.

Nonacceptance does not automatically cancel existing orders, forfeit payments, or remove valid product claims.

Current version

Each updated version will carry an effective date or version number. AERA will make the applicable terms available to resellers and retain records of the versions accepted.

Resellers should keep a copy of the terms and order confirmations applicable to their purchases.

Review and resolution

AERA and the reseller will first seek to resolve concerns through good-faith discussion.

AERA will review the information provided, request clarification where reasonably necessary, and communicate the proposed resolution or next steps in writing. If further investigation or courier coordination is required, AERA will provide an update and an estimated response timeline.

Any agreed resolution that changes an order, payment obligation, or other material arrangement must be recorded in writing and confirmed by both parties.

Applicable policies

Voluntary cancellations are governed by Section 4. Shipping concerns are addressed under Section 5, product claims under Section 6, and privacy requests under Section 14.

Submitting a concern does not automatically cancel an order or suspend payment obligations. Any temporary adjustment must be agreed in writing, without limiting rights available under applicable law.

Further remedies

If a concern cannot be resolved through discussion, either party may seek mediation, assistance from an appropriate authority, or other remedies available under applicable Philippine law.

Nothing in these terms requires either party to give up a legal right or prevents a party from seeking urgent relief where necessary.
